03 — Thinking

How I See It.

01

Most marketing is cowardly.

Safe copy, safe campaigns, safe results. The brands that actually break through have a real point of view and the discipline to hold it under pressure. Everything else is noise with a budget.

02

AI doesn't replace people who think.

It replaces people who don't. The prompt is the product — and most people haven't figured that out yet. The ones who have are building something that compounds.

03

The best work lives at the intersection.

Strategy without execution is theory. Execution without strategy is noise. The people who can hold both at once are rare. That's the lane I operate in.
